Start here

  1. Make your key. Open the menu (top left) and press Make my key. It is your stamp and it never leaves this computer.
  2. Try a plug. Click one in the Library (left), then press Allow. It runs here, locked in its own box.
  3. Add your own. Drop a file or folder anywhere, or press the button. It is checked first, in plain words.

Each plug in My plugs has an on/off switch. Link two plugs in the Links tab. Press ? for keys.

Ctrl+S saves. Your change is checked, then sealed with your key.
